Viewer 2 rules (curator decisions)

  • Extracellular molecules are never merged across models (26 Nov 2026). A synaptic cleft belongs to its own synapse: a molecule released at one synapse is not the one received at another. Each extracellular molecule (located in extracellular region GO:0005576 or a subterm, incl. synaptic cleft) stays in its own model and is joined only where that model uses the same molecule (e.g. glutamate released by the SNARE complex and sensed by GRM6 in one model). An extracellular molecule touching only one unit is drawn once for that unit. To be narrowed case by case.
  • Cell types: the first CL in the CC part_of chain; a cell part_of another cell (ON-bipolar cell part_of cone retinal bipolar cell) is one combined cell type. Units in different cell types never merge.
  • Unit merge: same gene product(s) + MF + BP (with its part_of chain) + CC (with its part_of chain).
  • Sources: live production first, noctua-dev only for models not in production; refreshed daily.
